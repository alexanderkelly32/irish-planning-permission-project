# FOI request templates (step 4 — unresolved schemes only)

Standard basis: Freedom of Information Act 2014, s.12; records held by the
authority in structured form; no plans or commercially sensitive documents
requested. One letter per authority; spreadsheet (xlsx/csv) requested, not
document copies. Fees: FOI is free for personal records; request records
"in the public interest" basis for organisational records.

---

## Template 1 — Part V + phasing schedules

To: FOI Officer, {Council}

Re: Freedom of Information Act 2014 — residential scheme records

I request, in spreadsheet form (xlsx or csv) where held:

1. For each planning application decided 2015-2024 that included a Part V
   obligation (application number, site address):
   - date of Part V proposal receipt; agreement-in-principle date; formal
     agreement date; number and type of units; agreed values; exemption
     certificate and reason; commencement date of Part V units; practical
     completion date; conveyance/lease/payment dates.
2. For each such application, the approved phasing schedule: phase number,
   phase description, number of houses and apartments per phase, house
   number ranges or block identifiers per phase, and any approved
   amendments to phasing.
3. Phase-specific completion records: date each phase was certified or
   taken in charge.

Personal data of individual applicants is not required; company names and
scheme-level fields suffice.

## Template 2 — Development contributions and bonds

To: FOI Officer, {Council}

I request, in spreadsheet form where held, for planning applications
decided 2015-2024:

- development contribution amount and payment date(s);
- bond/securities: lodgement amount and date, reduction dates, release
  date;
- commencement notices received and dates (where not in the national BCMS).

---

## Template 3 — Building control (supplementary to BCMS)

To: FOI Officer, {Council}

I request, in spreadsheet form where held, for commencements 2014-2024:

- inspection log dates per commencement notice (date of each inspection,
  without inspector names);
- Fire Safety Certificate (FSC) application, grant and revised-grant dates;
- Disability Access Certificate (DAC) application and grant dates;
- dates of any Section 11 information requests and responses.

---

## Template 4 — Roads (road-opening licences)

To: FOI Officer, {Council} Roads Section

I request, in spreadsheet form where held, for 2015-2024:

- road-opening licence: application date, location/road, approved works
  window, applicant, contractor, utility, reinstatement date, extensions.

---

## Template 5 — HSA (national, separate body)

To: FOI Officer, Health and Safety Authority

I request, in spreadsheet form where held, for construction projects
2015-2024 notified via AF1/AF2:

- notification date; planned start and end dates; duration; client name;
  PSDP and PSCS organisation names; site location (address or coordinates);
  any amendment dates. (No personal data of individuals requested.)

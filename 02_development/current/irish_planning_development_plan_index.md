# Irish planning-policy document index — Republic of Ireland

**Checked:** 20 September 2026  
**Scope:** the 31 local planning authorities in the Republic of Ireland — 26 county councils, three city councils, and two city-and-county councils. Northern Ireland is outside this index because it has a separate planning-authority structure.

## What this index covers

The links below are the official entry points for each authority's statutory **Development Plan**: the core local planning-policy document used when determining planning applications. Plan portals normally contain the written statement, zoning/settlement maps, appendices, Record of Protected Structures, SEA/AA material, and adopted variations.

For a specific site, also check the applicable Local Area Plan, any adopted variation, and the site-specific zoning map. A proposed variation is not treated here as operative policy unless the council has made/adopted it.

“Oldest confirmed” means the earliest county/city development-plan period that I could confirm was available online from an official council source during this review. It is not a claim that no older paper or local-library copy exists.

## National cross-check

Use [MyPlan.ie](https://www.myplan.ie/) to cross-check current zoning and policy mapping nationally. It is very useful for the live spatial view, but councils’ own plan portals remain the authoritative source for the complete written plans, maps, appendices and historical documents.

## Gaps and next retrieval step

Several councils publish excellent archives; others expose older material only through individual document URLs, an older site, or local records. Where this index says that an archive was not identified, that is a discovery limitation, not evidence that the council has no historic copies. For a complete historical corpus, ask the relevant Planning Office or County/City Library for electronic copies of superseded development plans and map books, specifying the plan periods you need.

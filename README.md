# Irish Planning Intelligence

**Turning 80 GB of Irish planning records into predictive intelligence.**

An end-to-end machine learning platform trained on hundreds of thousands of Irish planning applications, with records updated to August 2026.

**Validated on unseen applications from 2025**, the refusal model achieves an ROC-AUC of 0.779, with predicted refusal rates within two percentage points of observed rates for 98% of applications.

Given a proposed development's location, size, type and other characteristics, the platform predicts the probability of planning refusal, further information requests, appeals, decision delays and likely grant conditions, including confidence intervals.

It also retrieves comparable historical developments, extracting structured insights from planning documents to identify common refusal grounds, approval conditions and obstacles encountered by similar projects.

The objective is to turn an enormous, fragmented archive of planning documents into a practical tool for quantifying development risk before committing capital.

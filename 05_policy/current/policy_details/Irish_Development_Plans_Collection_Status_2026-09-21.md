## Collection boundary

This is a partial handoff, not a claim of a complete national corpus. The discovery manifest contains 2,740 eligible county/city Development Plan candidates from 28 directly accessible authorities (2005 onward). Sligo, Tipperary and Wicklow could not be collected in this environment because their public portals blocked retrieval; see `access_limitations.csv`.

`verified_document_inventory.csv` is the one-row-per-supplied-document index: it maps every ZIP member to its council source page, direct source URL, final URL, file size and SHA-256. Each supplied ZIP also carries the matching subset manifest.
